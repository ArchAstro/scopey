# Community code of conduct

Scopey contributors and maintainers are expected to make participation
welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while addressing the substance of disagreements.
- Respect differing experiences, identities, and levels of expertise.
- Keep reviews focused on the work, not the person.
- Protect private information, especially prompts, transcripts, and security
  reports.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, sustained disruption, and publication of another
person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. Report conduct
concerns privately to an ArchAstro organization owner through GitHub. Security
vulnerabilities should use the separate process in `SECURITY.md`.
