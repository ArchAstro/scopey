# Exact spectra of rational matrices

## Claimed theorem

Every matrix in `M_5(Q)` has five eigenvalues that can be expressed using a
finite combination of rational numbers, arithmetic operations, and radicals.

## Claimed proof

The characteristic polynomial has rational coefficients and degree five. The
quadratic, cubic, and quartic formulas show that polynomial roots can be
obtained by repeatedly adjoining radicals. Applying the same elimination steps
to the characteristic polynomial proves the result.

The supplied matrix is presented as a representative nontrivial example.
